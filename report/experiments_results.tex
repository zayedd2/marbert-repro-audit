\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:results-setup}

\fillin{Runs and compute: settings as in Section~\ref{sec:method}; seeds \rsSeeds{}; one \rsGPU{} on Colab;
\rsGPUHours{} GPU-hours in total (ASTD \rsGPUHoursAstd{}, ArSarcasm-Sent \rsGPUHoursArSarc{}, AJGT \rsGPUHoursAjgt{});
fp16 and dynamic padding as the speed choices.
Test sets: \rsNTestArSarc{}, \rsNTestAstd{} and \rsNTestAjgt{} tweets. Historical runs train on
\rsNTrainHistArSarc{}, \rsNTrainHistAstd{} and \rsNTrainHistAjgt{} tweets; independent runs hold out
\rsNDevArSarc{}, \rsNDevAstd{} and \rsNDevAjgt{} dev tweets.
Selection: historical = best test epoch by the published metric; independent = best dev epoch by the published
metric (dev F1-PN, dev accuracy for AJGT); ties to the earlier epoch; all metrics read at that one checkpoint.
Uncertainty: seed mean $\pm$ SD (training randomness) and a 95\% paired bootstrap over test tweets with
\rsBootB{} resamples (test-sample randomness), reported separately.
Reproduction rule from the proposal: gap $\le$ \rsGapThreshold{} points or published score inside the seed range,
and MARBERT $>$ AraBERT on that dataset.}
